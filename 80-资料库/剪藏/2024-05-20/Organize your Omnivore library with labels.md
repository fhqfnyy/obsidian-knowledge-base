## Highlights

> Omnivore provides labels (also known as tags) to help you organize your library. Labels can be added to any saved read, and your library can be filtered based on labels.  
> Omnivore 提供标签（也称为标记）来帮助您组织图书馆。可以将标签添加到任何已保存的读数中，并且可以根据标签过滤您的库。  [⤴️](https://omnivore.app/me/organize-your-omnivore-library-with-labels#439586d1-2a9d-4fb2-af6d-a67d6eb22017)  ^439586d1
