### III. Water Hammer Effect Calculation (Using Sudden Valve Closure as an Example)
### 三、 水锤效应计算（以阀门突然关闭为例）

#### (1) **Calculation of Peak Water Hammer Pressure (P_max)**
#### (1) **水锤压力峰值 (P_max) 计算**

**Formula (Direct Water Hammer):**
**公式 (直接水锤)：**
> **P_max = P₀ + (ρ * c * v)**
> **Where:**
> *   P₀ = Initial pressure = 0.35 MPa
> *   ρ = Density of water = 999 kg/m³
> *   c = Water hammer wave speed = 1460 m/s
> *   v = Flow velocity in the pipe (m/s)
> **其中：**
> *   P₀ = 初始压力 = 0.35 MPa
> *   ρ = 水的密度 = 999 kg/m³
> *   c = 水锤波速 = 1460 m/s
> *   v = 管道中水流速度 (m/s)

**Calculation Steps:**
**计算步骤：**
1.  **Calculate Flow Velocity (v):**
    1.  **计算水流速度 (v)：**
    *   Q₀ = 0.5 m³/s
    *   A = π * (D/2)² = π * (0.6/2)² ≈ 0.2827 m²
    *   **v = Q₀ / A = 0.5 / 0.2827 ≈ 1.769 m/s**

2.  **Calculate Peak Water Hammer Pressure (P_max):**
    2.  **计算水锤压力峰值 (P_max)：**
    > P_max = 0.35 MPa + (999 kg/m³ * 1460 m/s * 1.769 m/s)
    > = 0.35 MPa + 2.647 MPa
    > **≈ 3.0 MPa**

#### (2) **Calculation of Pressure Fluctuation Amplitude (ΔP)**
#### (2) **压力波动幅度 (ΔP) 计算**

> **ΔP = ρ * c * v = 2.647 MPa** (As calculated above)
> **ΔP = ρ * c * v = 2.647 MPa** (如上计算)

#### (3) **Calculation of Pressure Wave Propagation Time (t)**
#### (3) **压力波传播时间 (t) 计算**

> **t = L / c = 2500 m / 1460 m/s ≈ 1.712 s**

#### (4) **Estimation of Pressure Recovery Time (t_r) (Considering Pipe Elasticity)**
#### (4) **压力恢复时间 (t_r) 估算 (考虑管道弹性)**

*   **Theoretical Formula:** t_r = (L / (c * k)), where k is the elasticity coefficient of the pipe (PE pipes are relatively stiff, resulting in a larger k value and shorter t_r).
*   **理论公式：** t_r = (L / (c * k))，其中 k 是管道的弹性系数 (PE管较硬，k值较大，t_r较短)。
*   **Simplified Treatment:** For PE pipes, it is often considered that the peak water hammer pressure reaches its maximum value within **approximately 1.5 seconds** after valve closure, followed by rapid attenuation. In this example, **t_r ≈ 2.0 s** is adopted (precise calculation is required based on actual pipe parameters).
*   **简化处理：** 对于PE管，常认为水锤压力峰值在阀门关闭后**约1.5秒内**即可达到最大值，后续衰减较快。本例中取 **t_r ≈ 2.0 s** (实际需根据管材参数精确计算)。

### VI. Recommendations for Water Hammer Protection Measures

| **防护措施** | **具体措施** | **预期效果** | **实施优先级** |
| **1. Slow-closing Check Valve** | Install a **slow-closing check valve** (e.g., MAGNUM series) at the pump outlet. Set closing time to **2-3 seconds**. | Effectively limits peak water hammer pressure (P_max ≈ P₀ + ρ*c*v*(1-t/T), where T=slow closing time), reducing ΔP to a safe range. | **Highest Priority** |
| **1. 缓闭止回阀 (Safety Valve)** | 在水泵出口处安装**缓闭止回阀** (例如：MAGNUM系列)。关闭时间设为 **2-3秒**。 | 有效限制水锤峰值 (P_max ≈ P₀ + ρ*c*v*(1-t/T) ，T=缓闭时间)，降低ΔP至安全范围。 | **最高优先级** |
| **2. Air Chamber Buffer** | Install a **large air chamber** (Volume > 0.5 m³) at high points of the pipeline or at valves. | Absorbs part of the pressure pulse, reducing the amplitude of system pressure fluctuations. | **High Priority** |
| **2. 气室缓冲 (Air Chamber)** | 在管道高点或阀门处安装**大型气室** (容积 > 0.5 m³)。 | 吸收部分压力脉冲，降低系统压力波动幅度。 | **高优先级** |

| **3. 降低水速 (V)** | 调整系统运行，**降低设计流量**至1200 m³/h，使水速降至 **1.4 m/s**。 | 直接降低 ΔP = ρ*c*v，减轻水锤冲击。 | **中优先级** |
| **4. Pipe Material Optimization** | Consider **thickening PE pipe walls** (e.g., DN600, PE100, wall thickness ≥ 18mm) or switching to **steel pipes** (higher c, but recalculation required). | Increases pipe pressure capacity; steel pipes have c≈1200-1400 m/s (slightly lower than PE pipes, but higher strength). | **Medium Priority** |
| **4. 管道材料优化** | 考虑**加厚PE管壁** (如DN600, PE100, 壁厚≥18mm) 或改用**钢管** (c更高，但需重新计算)。 | 提高管道承受压力能力；钢管c≈1200-1400 m/s (比PE管略低，但强度高)。 | **中优先级** |
