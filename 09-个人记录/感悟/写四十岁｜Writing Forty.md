写四十岁｜Writing Forty

四十岁的门槛，横在人生的中央，
儿女尚未立，高堂体渐忧。
The threshold of forty stands across the middle of life:
children are not yet established, and parents' health quietly wanes.

斩志低头，亲友难聚首。
人性看透，万事封于口。
Ambitions are trimmed, the head bows lower;
friends and kin are harder to gather.
Human nature has been seen through,
and countless matters are sealed behind closed lips.

时而与岁月推杯换盏，
向命运索取一个掷地有声的答案；
At times, we raise our cups with the years,
pressing fate for an answer that strikes the ground with force.

时而在热闹喧嚣中褪色，
笑看人情世故的肥沃。
At times, amid noise and bustle, we fade,
smiling as we watch the fertile ground of human affairs.

三十尚可立，四十不惧秋。
At thirty, one may still strive to stand;
at forty, there is no need to fear autumn.

人生这场孤身赴会的宴席，
并非你一个人的残局。
Life, this banquet we attend alone,
is not a dead end meant only for you.

所以四十而已，莫愁千里路，
自有到来风。
So forty is only forty—do not grieve the long road ahead;
the winds that carry you forward will arrive in their time.

回头看看，那些被棘荆吻过的伤，
都是你脚步铿锵趟出来的勋章，
Look back: those wounds kissed by thorns
are medals forged by your resolute steps,

纵使你已鬓角染霜。
even though frost has touched your temples.

也在对人生校准航向，
已是土埋半身骨，何俱岁月蹉跎苦，
何俱岁月蹉磨风雨来宿。
You are already recalibrating life's course;
half-buried in the earth, bones deep in soil—
why fear the bitterness of time misspent,
why fear the grinding winds and rains of the years?

这具肉身在人间本身是必将散场的旅程，
那魂归地府不过是倦客还乡。
This flesh, living in the human world,
is itself a journey destined to end;
and when the soul returns to the underworld,
it is only a weary traveler going home.
