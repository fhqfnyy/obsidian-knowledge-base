### 醒了吗? Are you [[awake]]?

### 不舒服吗? Are you feeling sick?

1. Are you feeling sick?

### 睡得好吗? Did you sleep well?

1. Did you sleep well? (睡得好吗?)
2. Yes, I slept very well. (嗯，睡得挺好。)
3. Did you sleep well? (睡得好吗?)
4. No, I couldn't fall asleep. (哪儿啊，几乎没睡着。)

### 帮我关掉闹钟吗?
