# 《英语培训（上、下）》内容整理

## 一、课程概览

- 课程主题：**Business Etiquette（商务礼仪）**，副标题为 **For Business English (Greeting and Introduction)**。
- 核心模块：商务招待、英国宴请礼仪、介绍顺序与称谓、握手、名片交换、办公室接待、乘车礼仪、商务初次见面与再次见面的常用对话。
- 视频时长：上集约 41 分 27 秒；下集约 35 分 60 秒。
- 说明：以下英文按视频画面与语音综合整理。课件中存在少量拼写或语法问题；正文先还原其主要意思，再在“语言知识点”中给出推荐表达。

## 二、主要内容总结

### 1. 商务招待（Business Entertainment / Hospitality）

商务招待不仅包括正式宴会，也包括办公室茶水、工作餐、陪同客户观看活动等。它既是礼仪，也是维护客户关系和企业形象的一种投资。成功的商务招待应兼顾商务目的、客户感受、成本控制与文化差异。

在英国，受邀者应关注邀请方式、回复时效、赴宴地点、礼物和着装：

- 小型活动可电话回复；较正式或大型活动最好书面回复。
- 去别人家用餐，通常可带鲜花或巧克力；若与主人很熟，也可先询问是否需要带酒。
- 不确定着装时，应直接问主人或同行者。
- 正式晚宴或高档餐厅通常要求较正式的服装。

### 2. 介绍礼仪（The Art of Introduction）

基本原则是把相对年轻、职位较低或身份较次要的人介绍给年长、职位较高或身份更重要的人。例如：年轻者介绍给年长者、客人介绍给主人、初级主管介绍给高级主管。介绍时应使用恰当称谓，说明对方的身份或与场合的关系，并把姓名说慢、说清楚。

### 3. 握手礼仪（Handshakes）

保持合适距离，伸右手，掌心相触后握住；一般轻握、上下晃动不超过三次。握手时身体可略微前倾，保持目光接触和微笑，同时说 “Nice to meet you.” 或 “How do you do?”。双手拿满东西、手很脏，或对方身份极高而未主动伸手时，可视情况不主动握手。

### 4. 名片礼仪（Business Cards）

名片通常包括姓名、职务、公司和联系方式。递名片时可用双手持上方两角，使文字朝向对方；接到名片后应先阅读、确认发音或信息，再妥善放入名片夹或放在桌面上。不要立即塞进口袋，更不要放进后裤袋。

### 5. 办公室接待与乘车礼仪

办公室应有足够座位；访客到来时即使正在接电话，也应起身或用手势致意并请其落座。主动安置外套、提供合适杯具的茶水、避免无必要地接听其他电话。会谈结束后起身、握手、道别，并送客到门口。

乘车座次与上下车顺序要考虑车辆类型、司机身份和职位高低；核心是让主宾坐在更安全、舒适、方便的位置，并由接待方主动引导。

## 三、语言知识点、例句与翻译

### 0. 语音讲解补充：机场接人与行程确认

上集开头复习了商务接机的完整时间流程：事前确认行程；机场辨认和问候；自我介绍；询问旅途；引导前往停车场；主动帮助拿行李；在车上或到达后再次确认后续安排。

- **I’d like to confirm/check your flight number and arrival date.** 我想确认一下您的航班号和抵达日期。
- **I’m writing to inquire about your travel arrangements.** 我写信是想询问您的行程安排。
- **Are you Mr. Smith from ABC Company?** 请问您是 ABC 公司的 Smith 先生吗？
- **I’m Tom from the Purchasing Department.** 我是采购部的 Tom。
- **I’m in charge of receiving overseas clients.** 我负责接待海外客户。
- **How was your journey/flight?** 您旅途／飞行怎么样？
- **Please follow me. / This way, please.** 请跟我来。／请这边走。
- **Our car is in the underground parking lot.** 我们的车在地下停车场。
- **Let me help you with your bag/suitcase.** 我来帮您拿包／行李箱。
- **Let me go over the schedule for the next few days with you.** 我来和您过一下接下来几天的日程。
- **Would you like to go sightseeing if time permits?** 如果时间允许，您想去观光吗？

词语辨析：`confirm` 强调正式确认；`check` 更通用、更口语；`inquire about` 表示正式询问。`journey` 指整个旅程，`flight` 专指飞行。`bag` 是不确定行李种类时最稳妥的泛称；`suitcase` 指旅行箱，`briefcase` 指公文包。课上语音识别出的 `luggage` 才是“行李”的正确拼写。

### 1. welcome、meet 与 introduce

- **Welcome to our city/company.** 欢迎来到我们的城市／公司。
- **It’s a pleasure to meet you.** 很高兴认识您。
- **I’m pleased to meet you again.** 很高兴再次见到您。
- **Allow me to introduce my colleague, Mr. Wang.** 请允许我介绍一下我的同事王先生。
- **May I introduce Mr. Lee, a friend of mine, to you?** 我可以向您介绍我的朋友李先生吗？
- **Let me introduce myself. My name is Chen Wei.** 请允许我自我介绍一下，我叫陈伟。

知识点：`introduce A to B` 表示“把 A 介绍给 B”；正式场合可用 `Allow me to...` 或 `May I...?`，比直接说 `This is...` 更郑重。

### 2. 初次见面与再次见面

- **How do you do?** 您好／幸会。（正式、初次见面）
- **Nice to meet you.** 很高兴认识您。（初次见面）
- **Nice to see you again.** 很高兴再次见到您。（再次见面）
- **Great to see you again.** 再见到你真高兴。（较熟悉、较口语）
- **I’ve heard a lot about you.** 我久闻您的大名。
- **I’ve been looking forward to meeting you for a long time.** 我一直期待与您见面。

易错点：课件出现 `look forward to meet`，标准用法是 `look forward to + 名词/动名词`，因此应为 `look forward to meeting`。

### 3. 寒暄与近况

- **How are things?** 最近怎么样？
- **How are you doing?** 你最近怎么样？
- **How’s it going?** 近来如何？
- **What’s up?** 怎么样／最近有什么事？（非正式）
- **Not too bad.** 还不错。
- **Pretty good. How about you?** 挺好的，你呢？
- **Nothing much.** 没什么特别的。

### 4. 询问姓名与拼写

- **What was your name again? I beg your pardon.** 请问您刚才说您叫什么？抱歉我没听清。
- **By the way, how do you spell your name?** 顺便问一下，您的名字怎么拼？
- **Is Green your family name?** Green 是您的姓吗？
- **My family name is Green, and my given name is Tom.** 我姓 Green，名叫 Tom。

知识点：`family name / last name / surname` 都可表示“姓”；`given name / first name` 表示“名”。

### 5. 职务与工作

- **This is Ms. Johnson, our sales manager.** 这是我们的销售经理 Johnson 女士。
- **I work in the HR department.** 我在人力资源部工作。
- **I’m on the marketing consultancy team.** 我是市场咨询团队的一员。
- **What exactly do you do?** 您具体从事什么工作？
- **How many people do you employ in your office?** 你们办公室有多少名员工？

知识点：课件中的 `HR Office / Sales Office` 可以理解，但现代商务英语中通常说 `HR department / Sales department` 更自然。

### 6. 接待、饮品与落座

- **Please take a seat.** 请坐。
- **What would you like to drink?** 您想喝点什么？
- **I’d like some coffee, please.** 请给我一些咖啡。
- **Would you care for some tea?** 您想喝点茶吗？
- **Make yourself comfortable.** 请随意／请放松。

### 7. 邀请、回复与礼物

- **Thank you for inviting me.** 谢谢您的邀请。
- **I’d be delighted to come.** 我很乐意参加。
- **I’m afraid I won’t be able to attend.** 很遗憾，我不能出席。
- **Can I bring a bottle?** 我需要带一瓶酒来吗？
- **Please give my best regards to your family.** 请代我向您的家人致以诚挚问候。
- **My wife asked me to thank you for the gift. She liked it very much.** 我妻子让我感谢您送的礼物，她非常喜欢。

### 8. 差旅与日程

- **How was your flight?** 飞行旅途怎么样？
- **There were no holdups at the airport.** 机场没有耽搁。
- **I was able to take a nap on the way over.** 我在来的路上睡了一小会儿。
- **Do you have any jet lag?** 你有时差反应吗？
- **I’ve adjusted to the time difference.** 我已经适应时差了。
- **Your schedule is very flexible.** 您的日程安排很灵活。
- **The only firm appointments are a factory visit tomorrow and a half day at the office on Thursday.** 唯一确定的安排是明天参观工厂，以及周四在办公室待半天。

### 9. 握手与名片

- **Please allow me to give you my card.** 请允许我递上我的名片。
- **May I have your business card?** 可以给我一张您的名片吗？
- **How should I pronounce your name?** 您的名字应该怎么读？
- **It’s a pleasure doing business with you.** 很高兴与您合作。

### 10. 语音讲解中的其他实用接待表达

- **We have enough seats for all the visitors.** 我们为所有来访者准备了足够的座位。
- **There is a no-smoking sign in the meeting room.** 会议室里有禁烟标志。
- **I’ll ask Xiao Wang to see you out.** 我会让小王送您出去。
- **Are you allergic to seafood?** 您对海鲜过敏吗？
- **Do you have any dietary restrictions?** 您有任何饮食禁忌吗？
- **Would you prefer Chinese food or Western food?** 您更喜欢中餐还是西餐？
- **This is a local speciality that you may not find elsewhere.** 这是当地特色，在别处可能吃不到。
- **I’ve already booked a hotel for you.** 我已经为您订好酒店了。
- **You must be exhausted after such a long journey.** 长途旅行后您一定很疲惫。
- **Let me arrange a translator/guide for you.** 我来为您安排一名翻译／向导。
- **I’ll show you around the company.** 我带您参观一下公司。
- **I would suggest visiting the factory first.** 我建议先参观工厂。
- **Thank you for your hospitality.** 感谢您的热情款待。
- **Have a pleasant journey.** 祝您旅途愉快。

易错点：应说 `be allergic to seafood`，不是课上语音中的 `alert to seafood`；`suggest` 后常接动名词（`suggest visiting`）或 `suggest that...`，不宜说 `suggest you to visit`；`prefer A to B` 表示“比起 B 更喜欢 A”。

## 四、PPT 主要英文内容与中文翻译

### A. Background Reading — How to Be Entertained

**English (screen content, lightly corrected for OCR):**

> “To entertain” means to give hospitality as well as to interest and amuse. If people come to your country, will you know how to look after them? Will you know what kind of invitation to give them? Will you invite the visitors to your home or to a restaurant? Will you invite them to stay in your home or arrange for them to stay in a nearby hotel? Will you expect them to bring a present for you?

**中文：**

“To entertain” 不仅指使人感兴趣、愉快，也指款待客人。如果有人来到你的国家，你知道怎样照顾他们吗？你知道应该以什么方式邀请他们吗？你会请客人到家里还是餐厅？你会请他们住在家中，还是安排附近的酒店？你会期待他们带礼物吗？

**English:**

> Ways of showing hospitality are different in every country, and I have decided to concentrate on “how to be entertained” in Britain.

**中文：**

每个国家表达好客的方式都不同，这里重点介绍在英国接受款待时应注意什么。

**English:**

> People might invite you to their home or to go out with them for the evening. They might invite you when they see you or by telephone. However, it is still quite common to be invited by letter or by invitation card. The invitation will tell you what the occasion is, when it is and, occasionally, what you should wear.

**中文：**

人们可能邀请你到家中做客，或晚上一起外出。他们可能见面时邀请，也可能打电话邀请。不过，以信件或请柬正式邀请仍很常见。邀请中会说明活动是什么、何时举行，有时还会注明着装要求。

**English:**

> Reply quickly. If the occasion is small, you can reply by telephone, but if it is a big occasion, it is better to reply by letter. It is kinder to do this because it helps your host to remember and organise things. If the invitation was by formal invitation, you might like to reply in a formal way.

**中文：**

应尽快回复。小型活动可以电话回复，大型活动最好回信。这样能帮助主人确认人数并安排事务。若收到的是正式请柬，也宜以正式方式回复。

**English:**

> In Britain, it is common to be invited to someone’s home rather than to a restaurant. If you are invited to a restaurant, it may be because your host is very busy, cannot cook very well, wants to give you the enjoyment of a special type of food, or has often invited you home and now wants to give you a special treat.

**中文：**

在英国，被邀请到别人家中通常比去餐厅更常见。如果主人请你去餐厅，可能是因为很忙、不擅长做饭、希望你品尝某种特色食物，或已多次请你到家中，这次想给予特别款待。

**English:**

> Business visitors from other countries are often invited to their British colleagues’ homes. However, sometimes it may be more convenient to invite a business colleague to a restaurant.

**中文：**

来自其他国家的商务访客常被英国同事邀请到家中，但有时请商务同事去餐厅会更方便。

**English:**

> If you are invited to someone’s home to have a meal, it is usual to take some flowers or a box of chocolates. It is not usual to take a bottle of wine unless you know your host well. Although flowers and chocolates are quite acceptable, it would be very nice if you could take something from your country.

**中文：**

如果受邀到别人家吃饭，通常可以带鲜花或一盒巧克力。除非与主人很熟，否则一般不会直接带酒。鲜花和巧克力都合适，如果能带一件来自自己国家的小礼物会更贴心。

**English:**

> It is different if you are invited to a party, and it also depends on what kind of party it is. Many parties these days are “bring a bottle” parties. Unless your host is very rich, a bottle of wine is always welcome if it is a big party of, say, thirty or more people. Ask whether you should bring a bottle when you are invited if you are not sure. Say something like, “Can I bring a bottle?”

**中文：**

参加聚会时情况有所不同，还要看聚会类型。现在许多聚会是“自带一瓶酒”的形式。若是三十人以上的大型聚会，带一瓶酒通常很受欢迎。不确定时，可在受邀时询问：“我需要带一瓶酒吗？”

**English:**

> If you are not sure what you should wear, ask your host or your friends. Usually dress is formal and individual in Britain for parties and for invitations to dinner in people’s homes. If you are going to an expensive restaurant, the men may have to wear a jacket and tie; jeans would not be allowed.

**中文：**

如果不确定穿什么，应询问主人或朋友。在英国参加聚会或到家中赴宴，通常会注意正式得体且体现个人风格。去高档餐厅时，男士可能需要穿西装外套、系领带，牛仔裤可能不被允许。

### B. The Art of Introduction

**English:**

> Basic rules of introduction order: introduce a less important or junior person to a more important or senior person — a younger person to an older person; a gentleman to a lady; a guest to a host; a junior executive to a senior executive.

**中文：**

介绍顺序的基本规则：把身份相对次要或资历较浅的人介绍给身份更重要或资历更深的人——年轻者介绍给年长者；男士介绍给女士；客人介绍给主人；初级主管介绍给高级主管。

**English:**

> Remember to use titles when introducing people: Mr., Mrs., Miss, Ms.; Doctor, Professor, Judge, President; Sister, Father; Lady, Lord, Sir; President, Premier, Mayor, General.

**中文：**

介绍他人时要使用恰当称谓：先生、夫人、小姐、女士；博士／医生、教授、法官、主席／总统；修女、神父；女士／女爵、勋爵、爵士；总统、总理、市长、将军等。

**English:**

> Explain who the people are when you introduce them. “This is John Williams, General Manager of our company. He has been loyally serving the company for more than twenty years and has made great contributions to its development and success.”

**中文：**

介绍时要说明对方是谁。“这是 John Williams，我们公司的总经理。他忠诚地为公司服务了二十多年，并为公司的发展与成功作出了巨大贡献。”

**English:**

> Say names slowly, clearly and distinctly. Remember names and their spelling.

**中文：**

姓名要说得缓慢、清楚、分明；要记住姓名及其拼写。

### C. Handshakes (most practical and universal)

**English (meaning restored):**

> Stand about 1.2 metres away from the other person (move nearer or sideward, not backward). Extend your right hand so that your forearm and upper arm form an approximately straight angle. Keep the thumb up and the four fingers straight and together. Touch palms before wrapping your fingers around the other person’s hand. Shake no more than three times and then separate. While shaking, lean forward a little, look into the other person’s eyes, smile, and say “Nice to meet you” or “How do you do?” Handshakes may not be needed when your hands are full or dirty, or when the other person is much higher-ranking.

**中文：**

与对方保持约 1.2 米距离（应靠近或侧移，不要后退）。伸出右手，使前臂与上臂大致成合适角度；拇指向上，其余四指并拢伸直。先掌心相触，再用手指握住对方的手。上下晃动不超过三次后松开。握手时稍微前倾，注视对方眼睛，面带微笑，并说“很高兴认识您”或“您好”。双手拿满东西、手很脏，或对方级别高很多等情形下，可以不主动握手。

### D. Business Card

**English (meaning restored):**

> Design of a business card: name, rank, company and contact information, such as telephone number, fax number, correspondence address and email.
>
> Exchange business cards at the beginning or end of an informal meeting. Present a card with two hands, holding it by the two upper corners. In some countries, it is considered an insult for a guest to be the first to offer a business card.
>
> When accepting a card, make a point of studying it, commenting on it and clarifying information before putting it away. Pause and take time to read it; use the opportunity to repeat the person’s name aloud.
>
> Place the business card you receive in a card case or notebook, or on the table in front of you. Never place it in a wallet that will be put in your back pocket.

**中文：**

名片应包括姓名、职务、公司及电话、传真、通信地址、电子邮箱等联系方式。非正式会面可在开始或结束时交换名片。递名片时用双手拿住上方两角；在某些文化中，客人先递名片可能被视为失礼。收到名片后应认真阅读、适当评论并核实信息，再收好；还可借机大声重复对方姓名以确认读音。名片应放入名片夹、笔记本或摆在面前的桌上，不要放进会塞到后裤袋的钱包。

### E. Scene 1 — 接待来访客户

- **Good morning, Mr. Smith. Welcome to our city.** 早上好，Smith 先生。欢迎来到我们的城市。
- **Good morning, Mr. Liu. This is Mr. Johnson, our sales manager.** 早上好，刘先生。这是我们的销售经理 Johnson 先生。
- **We met several years ago. How are you, Mr. Johnson?** 我们几年前见过。您好吗，Johnson 先生？
- **I’m very well, thank you. And you?** 我很好，谢谢。您呢？
- **I’m fine. I’m so pleased to meet you again. Please allow me to introduce my colleague, Mr. Wang.** 我很好。很高兴再次见到您。请允许我介绍一下我的同事王先生。
- **Glad to meet you, Mr. Wang.** 很高兴认识您，王先生。
- **Nice to see you, Mr. Wang.** 很高兴见到您，王先生。
- **I’m glad to meet you, too. Welcome to China.** 我也很高兴认识您。欢迎来到中国。
- **Take a seat, please. What would you like to drink?** 请坐。您想喝点什么？
- **Tea, please.** 请给我茶。
- **I’d like some coffee, please.** 请给我一些咖啡。
- **Did you have a nice trip?** 旅途愉快吗？
- **I enjoyed the trip very much, thank you.** 旅途很愉快，谢谢。
- **We hope you’ll also enjoy your stay here.** 希望您在这里也过得愉快。
- **I think we will. Thank you.** 我想会的，谢谢。

### F. Scene 2 — 介绍同事并谈工作

- **Hello, Tom. Nice to see you. How are things?** 你好，Tom。很高兴见到你，最近怎么样？
- **Fine, thanks. And you?** 很好，谢谢。你呢？
- **Oh, not too bad.** 嗯，还不错。
- **May I introduce you to Jack Brown?** 我可以把 Jack Brown 介绍给你吗？
- **How do you do? I’m Tom Smith. I work in the HR department.** 您好，我是 Tom Smith。我在人力资源部工作。
- **Pleased to meet you. I’m in the Sales Department.** 很高兴认识您。我在销售部工作。
- **And what exactly do you do?** 您具体做什么工作？
- **I’m part of the marketing consultancy team.** 我是市场咨询团队的一员。
- **How many people do you employ in your office?** 你们办公室有多少人？
- **Fifteen in all.** 一共十五人。
- **So how long have you been here?** 那么您在这里工作多久了？
- **I’m quite new. I only started at the beginning of the year.** 我来这里不久，今年年初才开始工作。
- **Well, best of luck.** 那祝你好运。

### G. Scene 3 — 机场重逢、飞行与日程

- **Paul! Great to see you again.** Paul！再次见到你真高兴。
- **Good to see you too, John. How was your flight?** 我也很高兴见到你，John。飞行旅途怎么样？
- **Good. There were no holdups at the airport, and the flight was comfortable. I was able to take a nap on the way over.** 很好，机场没有耽搁，飞行也很舒适。我在途中还睡了一小会儿。
- **Do you have any jet lag?** 你有时差反应吗？
- **Not now. I adjusted to the time difference while I was in Hong Kong, so I’ll be all right.** 现在没有。我在香港时已经适应了时差，所以没问题。
- **I always get bad jet lag when I travel.** 我旅行时总会有严重的时差反应。
- **How is your wife doing?** 你妻子近来好吗？
- **She’s doing fine, thank you. How is your family?** 她很好，谢谢。你的家人好吗？
- **Fine, thanks. My wife asked me to thank you for your gift last time. She liked it very much.** 都很好，谢谢。我妻子让我感谢你上次送的礼物，她非常喜欢。
- **I’m glad she liked it.** 我很高兴她喜欢。
- **I’m hoping to get some exercise and go sightseeing. Will we have time?** 我还希望锻炼一下并观光。我们有时间吗？
- **Of course. Your schedule is very flexible.** 当然，您的日程很灵活。
- **It’s good to be here and nice to meet you.** 很高兴来到这里，也很高兴见到你。
- **I’m glad to have you, too.** 我也很高兴接待你。

### H. Scene 4 — 核对身份、姓名与共同熟人

- **Excuse me, are you John Smith?** 请问，您是 John Smith 吗？
- **Yes, I am. And you are Mr…?** 是的。请问您是……？
- **Tom. Tom Green of ETP.** Tom，ETP 的 Tom Green。
- **Is Green your family name?** Green 是您的姓吗？
- **Yes. My last name is Green. My given name is Tom.** 是的，我姓 Green，名叫 Tom。
- **Glad to know you.** 很高兴认识您。
- **I’ve known your name for a long time.** 我早就听说过您的名字。
- **You know Bob Smith, don’t you?** 您认识 Bob Smith，对吧？
- **Do you know who he is?** 您知道他是谁吗？
- **Can’t you see that we look somewhat alike?** 您没看出我们长得有些像吗？
- **He is your relative, right?** 他是您的亲戚，对吗？
- **That’s right.** 对。
- **Please give him my best regards.** 请代我向他问好。
- **I’ll do that. Thank you very much.** 我会的，非常感谢。

### I. Sentence Patterns 与练习要求

**PPT 句型：**

- **Allow me to introduce myself. My name is…** 请允许我自我介绍，我叫……
- **Excuse me, aren’t you Mr./Ms. … from Brazil?** 请问，您是不是来自巴西的……先生／女士？
- **May I introduce Mr. Lee, a friend of mine, to you?** 我可以向您介绍我的朋友李先生吗？
- **Mr. President, I’m glad to introduce her to you.** 总裁先生，我很高兴把她介绍给您。
- **I’ve heard a lot about you.** 我久闻您的大名。
- **I’ve often heard of you.** 我经常听人提起您。
- **He always talks about you.** 他总是谈起您。
- **I’ve been looking forward to meeting you for a long time.** 我一直期待与您见面。

**PPT 练习场景：**

- **Meeting a client for the first time at the airport.** 第一次在机场会见客户。
- **Meeting a client for the first or second time in the company.** 在公司第一次或第二次会见客户。

## 五、课件中值得纠正或优化的表达

| 课件中的表达或 OCR 还原 | 推荐表达 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| `What would like to drink?` | `What would you like to drink?` | 缺主语 `you`。 |
| `I’ve been looking forward to meet you.` | `I’ve been looking forward to meeting you.` | `look forward to` 后接名词或动名词。 |
| `How to accepting...` | `How to accept...` / `When accepting...` | `how to` 后接动词原形。 |
| `I adjust to the time difference...` | `I adjusted to...` / `I’ve adjusted to...` | 叙述已发生的适应过程要用过去时或现在完成时。 |
| `I’m glad she like it.` | `I’m glad she liked it.` | 礼物是上次收到的，宜用过去时。 |
| `Glad to know you.` | `Glad to meet you.` / `Nice to meet you.` | 初次见面更自然。 |
| `How to spell your name?` | `How do you spell your name?` | 完整疑问句需要助动词。 |
| `a gentle man` | `a gentleman` | `gentleman` 是一个词。 |
| `other person’s eyes`（复数/单数混乱） | `the other person’s eyes` | 指当前握手对象时用单数所有格。 |
| `rank`（名片字段） | `job title / position` | 名片上表示职务时更自然。 |

## 六、复习提纲

1. 先记住三组核心功能句：欢迎与寒暄、介绍、接待饮品。
2. 区分初次见面 `meet` 与再次见面 `see ... again`。
3. 掌握介绍结构：`introduce A to B`，并遵循“次要者介绍给重要者”的礼仪顺序。
4. 熟悉姓名结构：`family/last name` 与 `given/first name`。
5. 用完整商务流程练习：机场迎接 → 相互介绍 → 寒暄旅途 → 安排日程 → 送客。
6. 将握手、名片、着装、礼物和办公室接待等非语言礼仪，与英语表达一起练习。
