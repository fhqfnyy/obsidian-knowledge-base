## 一、Work & Study 工作与学习

### 1. Do you work or are you a student?

I’m currently working as an electrical and automation engineer. My job mainly involves electrical systems, PLC programming, and industrial automation. I’ve been working in this field for many years, so I’m quite familiar with it. I enjoy the technical side of my job, especially solving problems.

### 2. What do you do for a living?

I work in electrical engineering and automation. A large part of my job is related to control systems, electrical equipment, and troubleshooting. Sometimes I also need to communicate with other engineers and operators on site. It can be busy, but I find it interesting.

### 3. Do you enjoy your job?

Yes, for the most part, I do. I like solving technical problems and seeing a system work properly after installation or commissioning. Of course, the job can sometimes be stressful, especially when there is an urgent fault, but overall I find it rewarding.

### 4. What is the most interesting part of your job?

The most interesting part is probably troubleshooting. Sometimes a system doesn’t work as expected, so I have to check the program, wiring, instruments, and equipment step by step. I enjoy the process of finding the real cause of a problem and finally fixing it.

### 5. What is difficult about your job?

One difficult part is dealing with unexpected problems. Equipment can fail at any time, and sometimes we have to solve the issue very quickly. Another challenge is communication, because different teams may have different ideas or priorities.

### 6. Would you like to change your job in the future?

At the moment, I’m quite satisfied with my field, so I don’t have any immediate plans to change it. However, I’d like to keep learning new technologies and perhaps move into more advanced automation or management work in the future.

### 7. Why did you choose this career?

I chose this career because I was interested in electrical systems and technology. I also like practical work and problem-solving. Engineering gives me the chance to use both technical knowledge and real-world experience, so it suits my personality quite well.

### 8. Do you prefer working alone or in a team?

I prefer a combination of both. When I’m programming or analyzing a technical problem, I like working alone because I can concentrate better. However, large engineering projects require teamwork, so good communication is also very important.

### 9. What skills are important in your job?

Technical knowledge is obviously important, but I think communication and problem-solving are just as important. Engineers often need to explain complicated issues clearly. Being patient and responsible is also necessary because small mistakes can sometimes cause bigger problems.

### 10. Do you want to learn more skills for your work?

Definitely. Technology changes quickly, so engineers need to keep learning. I’d like to improve my knowledge of artificial intelligence, industrial communication, and programming. I also want to improve my English because it’s very useful for technical documents and international communication.

# 万能30秒回答公式

雅思 Part 1 最好不要只说一句。

可以固定使用：

**直接回答 → 原因 → 细节/例子 → 补充感受**

例如：

**Do you like walking?**

Yes, definitely.  
I usually go for a walk in the evening because it helps me relax after work.  
I normally walk for about an hour, especially when the weather is nice.  
I think it’s one of the easiest ways to stay active.

这个结构基本可以套用大多数题目。

# 10个雅思口语万能句型

1. **Yes, definitely.**  
    当然。
    
2. **For the most part, yes.**  
    总体来说，是的。
    
3. **It really depends.**  
    这个要看情况。
    
4. **I wouldn’t say I’m really into it, but...**  
    我不能说自己特别喜欢，不过……
    
5. **The main reason is that...**  
    主要原因是……
    
6. **One thing I really like about it is...**  
    我特别喜欢它的一点是……
    
7. **I guess it’s because...**  
    我想可能是因为……
    
8. **Compared with the past,...**  
    和过去相比……
    
9. **If I had the chance, I’d...**  
    如果有机会，我会……
    
10. **I think the key is...**  
    我觉得关键在于……
    

# 最重要的练习方法

不要100题全部死记。

建议把每一道题先练成：

**第一遍：看着答案读。**

**第二遍：只看问题，复述大概意思。**

**第三遍：不用原答案，用自己的话说。**

例如原答案：

> I usually go for a walk in the evening because it helps me relax after work.

你完全可以改成：

> I normally take a walk after dinner. It helps me clear my mind and relax.

雅思真正需要的是这种**能自由替换表达的能力**，而不是一字不差地背答案。
