**六、没听清** **/** **没听懂时的应对句子**

**原则：** 听不清时不要硬猜。礼貌地请对方重复、放慢速度或换一种说法，是正常且专业的沟通。

|---|---|---|---|
|**序号**|**英文句子**|**中文意思**|**适用场景**|
|1|Sorry, could you repeat the question, please?|不好意思，可以请您再说一遍这个问题吗？|最万能，优先背|
|2|Could you say that again, please?|可以再说一遍吗？|更口语|
|3|Sorry, I didn’t catch that.|不好意思，我刚才没听清。|自然常用|
|4|Sorry, I didn’t catch the last part.|不好意思，我最后一部分没听清。|只漏听后半句|

# 第三部分：PLC / SCADA（19–27）

**19. What is a PLC?**

_PLC_ _是什么？_

**Answer:** A PLC is an industrial controller. It receives signals, processes the program logic and controls field equipment.

**20. What can a PLC control?**

_PLC_ _可以控制什么？_

**Answer:** A PLC can control pumps, motors, valves, VFDs and many other devices.

**21. What is SCADA?**

_SCADA_ _是什么？_

**Answer:** SCADA is used to monitor and control the system. Operators can see equipment status, alarms and process data.

**22. What is the difference between PLC and SCADA?**

_PLC_ _和_ _SCADA_ _有什么区别？_

**Answer:** The PLC directly controls the equipment. SCADA is mainly used for monitoring and operation.

**23. What PLC software do you use?**

_你使用什么_ _PLC_ _软件？_

**Answer:** I mainly use Siemens TIA Portal for PLC programming.

**24. What SCADA software do you use?**

_你使用什么_ _SCADA_ _软件？_

**Answer:** I mainly use Siemens WinCC.

**25. What communication protocols do you know?**

_你熟悉哪些通信协议？_

**Answer:** I am familiar with Profinet, Modbus TCP and Modbus RTU.

**26. What is an input signal?**

_什么是输入信号？_

**Answer:** An input signal gives information to the PLC, such as pressure, level, temperature or equipment status.

**27. What is an output signal?**

_什么是输出信号？_

**Answer:** An output signal is used by the PLC to control equipment, such as starting a pump or opening a valve.

# 第四部分：电气、VFD 和水泵（28–35）

**28. What is a VFD?**

_VFD_ _是什么？_

**Answer:** A VFD is used to control the speed of an AC motor by changing the frequency.

**29. Why do we use a VFD for a pump?**

_为什么水泵要使用变频器？_

**Answer:** We use a VFD to control the pump speed, flow or pressure. It can also save energy.

**30. How do you control water pressure?**

_如何控制水压？_

**Answer:** We measure the pressure with a pressure transmitter. The PLC compares the actual pressure with the set point and adjusts the pump speed through the VFD.

**31. What is a soft starter?**

_什么是软启动器？_

**Answer:** A soft starter reduces the starting current and starts the motor smoothly.

**32. What is the difference between a VFD and a soft starter?**

_变频器和软启动器有什么区别？_

**Answer:** A soft starter is mainly used for starting and stopping a motor. A VFD can also control the motor speed during operation.

**33. What is a circuit breaker used for?**

_断路器有什么作用？_

**Answer:** A circuit breaker is used to protect an electrical circuit from faults such as overload and short circuit.

**34. What is a transformer used for?**

_变压器有什么作用？_

**Answer:** A transformer is used to change the voltage level in an electrical system.

**35. Why is grounding important?**

_为什么接地很重要？_

**Answer:** Grounding is important for safety. It helps protect people and equipment when an electrical fault happens.

# 第五部分：故障处理（36–43）

**36. How do you troubleshoot a problem?**

_你如何排查故障？_

**Answer:** First, I check the alarm information. Then I check the power supply and PLC signals. After that, I check the field equipment. Finally, I find the cause and solve the problem.

**37. What would you do if a pump cannot start?**

_如果水泵无法启动，你会怎么做？_

**Answer:** First, I check the power supply. Then I check the protection signals and PLC conditions. After that, I check the motor and field equipment.

**38. What would you do if a PLC loses communication?**

_如果_ _PLC_ _通信中断，你会怎么处理？_

**Answer:** First, I check the network connection. Then I check the cable, switch and IP settings. After that, I check the PLC and communication configuration.

**39. What would you do if an instrument gives a wrong value?**

_如果仪表显示错误数值，你会怎么处理？_

**Answer:** First, I check the instrument itself. Then I check the power supply, wiring and signal. I also compare the value with the actual process condition.

**40. How do you find an electrical fault?**

_你如何查找电气故障？_

**Answer:** I check the system step by step, from the power supply to the control circuit and then to the field equipment.

**41. What do you do after solving a problem?**

_解决故障后你会做什么？_

**Answer:** After solving the problem, I test the equipment and make sure it is running normally.

**42. What do you do if you cannot solve a problem?**

_如果你无法解决问题怎么办？_

**Answer:** I check the drawings and manuals and discuss the problem with my team. If necessary, I contact the equipment supplier.

**43. How do you prevent the same problem from happening again?**

_如何防止同样的问题再次发生？_

**Answer:** We analyze the cause, improve the system if necessary and make a maintenance plan.
