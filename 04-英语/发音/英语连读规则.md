## 1.前辅后元直接连：
==**When i**== was young I'd listen to the radio.
==**whe(ni)**== was young I'd listen to the radio.
## 2.爆辅相连要失爆（p,b,t,d,k,g与辅音相遇，第一个辅音不发，只停留一个位置。发第二个辅音的音）
1. When i was young ==**I'd listen**== to the radio.
2. When i was young ==**I'（） listen**== to the radio.
## 3.两元相连要加音
  radio  =radi(ro)
  how I = Hao (wai)
## 4.相同辅音发后面
get to = geto
## 5.h ,th要击穿
so much has changed=so muchas changed.
