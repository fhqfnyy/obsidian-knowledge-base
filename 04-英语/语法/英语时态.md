# 英语时态

| 时间/动作状态 | 一般动作     | 进行动作            | 完成动作            | 完成进行动作                |
| ------- | -------- | --------------- | --------------- | --------------------- |
| 现在时间    | do/does  | am/is/are+doing | have/has+done   | have/has been doing   |
| 过去时间    | did      | was/were+doing  | had+ done       | had been doing        |
| 将来时间    | will+do  | will be+doing   | will have done  | will have been doing  |
| 过去将来时间  | would+do | would be+doing  | would have done | would have been doing |
 ## 一般现在时
 1. I walk to school every day.
 2. He walks to school every day.
 3. I go to school every day.
 4. He goes to school every day.
## 一般过去时
1. I/he walked to school yester day.
2. I/he went to school yester day.
## 一般将来时
 I/he will walk to school tomorrow.
## 一般过去将来时
He said he would walk to school the next day.
