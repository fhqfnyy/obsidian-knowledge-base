| 序号  |     介词      | 解释           | 例句                                                                                          |
| :-: | :---------: | :----------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  1  | in, inside  | 在...内部（空间位置） | The book is **in** the box. / She lives **inside** the building.                            |
|  2  |     on      | 在...表面（空间位置） | The cat is **on** the table. / The book is **on** the shelf.                                |
|  3  |     at      | 在...地点（具体位置） | I am **at** school. / The post office is **at** the corner.                                 |
|  4  |    near     | 靠近（距离较近）     | The park is **near** my house. / He lives **near** the station.                             |
|  5  |    under    | 在...下方（垂直位置） | The cat is **under** the table. / The book is **under** the chair.                          |
|  6  |    over     | 在...上方（垂直位置） | The plane is **over** the city. / The bridge is **over** the river.                         |
|  7  |    below    | 在...下方（水平位置） | The subway is **below** the street level. / The number is **below** 10.                     |
|  8  |    above    | 在...上方（水平位置） | The number is **above** 10. / The mountain is **above** the clouds.                         |
|  9  |   around    | 围绕（环绕）       | The children are playing **around** the garden. / The smell is **around** the room.         |
| 10  |   through   | 穿过（从内部通过）    | The river flows **through** the valley. / She walked **through** the forest.                |
| 11  |    among    | 在...之中（三者以上） | The prize was divided **among** the three winners. / They argued **among** themselves.      |
| 12  |   between   | 在...之间（两者之间） | The book is **between** the two chairs. / The choice is **between** options A and B.        |
| 13  |   behind    | 在...后面（位置）   | The car is **behind** the bus. / He is **behind** me in line.                               |
| 14  | in front of | 在...前面（位置）   | The teacher is **in front of** the class. / The picture is **in front of** the door.        |
| 15  |    along    | 沿着（路线方向）     | The road runs **along** the river. / We walked **along** the beach.                         |
| 16  |   across    | 横跨（从一边到另一边）  | The bridge is **across** the river. / She walked **across** the street.                     |
| 17  |   towards   | 向...方向（朝向）   | The dog ran **towards** the door. / The sun is rising **towards** the east.                 |
| 18  |  opposite   | 在...对面（相对位置） | The house is **opposite** the school. / The number 5 is **opposite** 10 on the number line. |
| 19  |    into     | 进入（进入某物内部）   | The cat jumped **into** the box. / He poured the water **into** the glass.                  |
| 20  |   out of    | 从...出来（离开某物） | She climbed **out of** the car. / The light is shining **out of** the window.               |
| 21  |     up      | 向上（方向）       | The ladder is leaning **up** against the wall. / The temperature is rising **up**.          |
| 22  |    down     | 向下（方向）       | The ladder is leaning **down** towards the ground. / The temperature is falling **down**.   |
