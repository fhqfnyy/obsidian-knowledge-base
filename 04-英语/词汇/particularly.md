#### particularly _/pəˈtɪkjʊləlɪ/_  CET4 TEM4
- 1. 
    
    ADV You use **particularly** to indicate that what you are saying applies especially to one thing or situation. 特别 [ADV with cl/group]
    
    例：
    
    Keep your office space looking good, particularly your desk.
    
    保持你办公的地方整洁美观，特别是办公桌。
    
    例：
    
    More local employment will be created, particularly in service industries.
    
    更多的面向当地的就业将被创造出来，尤其是在服务业。
    
- 2. 
    
    ADV **Particularly** means more than usual or more than other things. 格外; 异乎寻常地 [ADV with cl/group] [强调]
    
    例：
    
    Progress has been particularly disappointing.
    
    进展一直格外令人失望。
