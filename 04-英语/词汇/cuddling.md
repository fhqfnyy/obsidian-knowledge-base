柯林斯辨析词组同根词
#### cuddle _/ˈkʌdəl/_  TEM8 ( cuddling, cuddled, cuddles )
- 1. 
    
    V-T If you **cuddle** someone, you put your arms around them and hold them close as a way of showing your affection. 搂抱; 拥抱; 怀抱
    
    例：
    
    He cuddled the newborn girl.
    
    他怀抱着新生的女婴。
    
- 2. 
    
    N-COUNT **Cuddle** is also a noun. 拥抱
    
    例：
    
    It would have been nice to give him a cuddle and a kiss but there wasn't time.
    
    当时要是拥抱他并吻他一下就好了，可惜时间来不及。
