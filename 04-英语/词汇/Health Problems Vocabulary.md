# Health Problems Vocabulary

| 词汇                     | 中文解释                                                                 | 例句                                                                 |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Sprained ankle           | 踝关节扭伤                                                               | He twisted his ankle and got a sprained ankle.（他扭伤脚踝导致踝关节扭伤。） |
| Migraine                 | 偏头痛                                                                   | She often suffers from migraines.（她经常患偏头痛。）                   |
| Walker                   | 助行器（辅助行走的器具）                                                 | The elderly woman uses a walker to move safely.（这位老年女性使用助行器安全行走。） |
| Bruise                   | 瘀伤                                                                     | She fell and got a bruise on her knee.（她摔倒后膝盖出现了瘀伤。）       |
| mouth ulcer              | 口腔溃疡                                                                 | A mouth ulcer can make eating painful.（口腔溃疡会导致进食疼痛。）       |
| Blister                  | 水泡                                                                     | New shoes often cause blisters on the feet.（新鞋常导致脚上起水泡。）   |
| itching                  | 瘙痒                                                                     | Itching is a common symptom of insect bites.（瘙痒是虫咬的常见症状。）   |
| Sore eye                 | 眼睛酸痛                                                                 | Prolonged screen use may lead to sore eyes.（长时间看屏幕可能导致眼睛酸痛。） |
| Sling                    | 肢体吊带（用于固定受伤手臂等）                                           | After his shoulder injury, he used a sling for support.（肩部受伤后，他用吊带固定。） |
| Sore throat              | 喉咙痛                                                                   | Gargling with salt water helps with a sore throat.（盐水漱口可缓解喉咙痛。） |
| A Cough                  | 咳嗽（名词）                                                             | A persistent cough can be a sign of illness.（持续咳嗽可能是疾病的征兆。） |
| A Sneeze                 | 喷嚏                                                                     | He let out a loud sneeze during the meeting.（他在会议中打了一个响亮的喷嚏。） |
| A Cold                   | 感冒                                                                     | She took medicine to recover from a cold.（她服药以从感冒中恢复。）     |
| The Flu                  | 流感                                                                     | Getting a flu shot can prevent the flu.（接种流感疫苗可预防流感。）     |
| A Stuffy nose            | 鼻塞                                                                     | A stuffy nose often accompanies a cold.（鼻塞常伴随感冒出现。）         |
| A Earache                | 耳痛（注：正确表达应为 *An Earache*）                                    | Children often get earaches when they have colds.（孩子感冒时常会耳痛。） |
| A Headache               | 头痛                                                                     | Drinking water can help relieve a headache.（喝水有助于缓解头痛。）     |
| A Fever                  | 发烧                                                                     | A fever is the body's response to infection.（发烧是身体对感染的反应。） |
| Dizziness                | 头晕                                                                     | Dizziness can occur when you stand up too fast.（站起过快可能导致头晕。） |
| Sunburn                  | 晒伤                                                                     | Wearing sunscreen prevents sunburn.（涂抹防晒霜可预防晒伤。）           |
| Heart attack             | 心脏病发作                                                               | Chest pain is a common symptom of a heart attack.（胸痛是心脏病发作的常见症状。） |
| Vomiting                 | 呕吐                                                                     | Vomiting can be caused by food poisoning.（呕吐可能由食物中毒引起。）   |
| Rash                     | 皮疹                                                                     | Some people develop a rash from allergic reactions.（有些人因过敏反应出现皮疹。） |
| Suffocation              | 窒息                                                                     | Smoke inhalation can lead to suffocation.（吸入浓烟可能导致窒息。）     |
| Broken leg               | 断腿                                                                     | He was in a cast after breaking his leg.（他断腿后打了石膏。）           |
| Cold Sneeze              | 冷喷嚏（因寒冷引发的喷嚏）                                               | Stepping into cold air gave him a cold sneeze.（踏入冷空气时他打了个冷喷嚏。） |
| Dandruff                 | 头皮屑                                                                   | Dandruff is a common scalp condition.（头皮屑是常见的头皮问题。）       |
| Swelling                 | 肿胀                                                                     | Swelling around the injury indicates inflammation.（伤口周围肿胀表明有炎症。） |
| Allergic                 | 过敏的（形容词）                                                         | Many people are allergic to peanuts.（许多人对花生过敏。）               |
| Backache                 | 背痛                                                                     | Sitting with poor posture can cause a backache.（坐姿不良可能导致背痛。） |
