## coalesce #flashcard 
英 [ˌkəʊəˈles] 美 [ˌkoʊəˈles]
- v. <正式>合并，联合；使（元素）结合
[ 第三人称单数 coalesces 现在分词 coalescing 过去式 coalesced 过去分词 coalesced ]
柯林斯辨析词组同根词
<!--ID: 1716217640434-->


- 1. V-I If two or more things **coalesce**, they come together and form a larger group or system. 合并 [正式]
    例：
    As the number of moisture droplets increases, they begin to coalesce into small rivulets of fluid.
    当水滴数量增加，它们会汇集成细流。
    例：
    His sporting and political interests coalesced admirably in his writing about climbing.
    
    他对运动和政治的兴趣,很了不起地共同体现在他关于攀岩运动的写作中。
